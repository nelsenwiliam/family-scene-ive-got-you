A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for the **Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene** bounty:
a tiny pure-stdlib Python program that writes one warm, funny, heartfelt
family scene to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The scene

> Dad ran behind my bike yelling, "I've got you!" At the top of the hill I looked back — he was three houses behind, hands on his knees, still yelling, "I've got you!"
> And I believed him.

A dad's encouragement outrunning his legs: a vivid, funny visual with a
heartfelt landing in the second line — 185 characters, inside the
280-character cap, two lines.

Why this scene: it is a real family moment on the page (criterion 1), the
closing line lands with genuine affection rather than pasted sentiment (2),
the "three houses behind, hands on his knees" turn is a light, recognisable
laugh (3), both lines earn their place with no word wasted (4), and the
specific detail keeps it off greeting-card territory (5).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the scene to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when set). No third-party packages, no network, no secrets.
